Funding Opportunities

A community-curated list of grants, fellowships, and sponsorship programs relevant to academic open source, research software, and OSPO operations. Entries can be sorted and filtered below. There is also an RSS feed.

The initial set of entries is imported from the Johns Hopkins OSPO Funding Opportunities page, with thanks to the JHU OSPO team for maintaining and sharing it.

To suggest a new opportunity, use the Suggest an opportunity button below the list — it opens a GitHub issue you can fill out. Maintainers will review and add accepted entries via pull request.

NIH Research Software Engineer (RSE) Award

Grant
National Institutes of Health (NIH)

$300,000 for up to three years; no more than $200,000 for one year

Deadline: Letter of intent: 2026-11-04 · Full proposal: 2026-12-04

Salary support via the NIH Research Specialist (R50) mechanism for Research Software Engineers contributing to NIH-funded biomedical, clinical, behavioral, or health-related research software, tools, and algorithms. Targets RSEs outside a traditional independent-investigator career path.

Geography
USA
Focus area
Biomedical, clinical, behavioral, and health research software
Website
https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/rfa-files/RFA-OD-24-011.html

Pathways to Enable Secure Open-Source Ecosystems (PESOSE)

Grant
National Science Foundation (NSF)

Track 1 (Planning): up to $300,000 · Track 2 (Establishment): up to $1,500,000 · Track 3 (Security): up to $1,500,000; 40–60 awards anticipated

Deadline: Full proposal: 2027-03-02 · Full proposal: 2027-09-07 (first Tuesday in March and September, annually)

Successor to POSE (NSF 24-606). Supports turning promising open-source research products into sustainable, secure open-source ecosystems, via three tracks: planning and scoping a new ecosystem, establishing or expanding an ecosystem around a product with existing adoption, and enhancing the safety, security, and privacy of existing ecosystems. A prior planning award is not required for the other tracks.

Eligibility
See solicitation NSF 26-506.
Geography
USA
Focus area
Open-source research software ecosystems
Website
https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/pesose-pathways-enable-secure-open-source-ecosystems

GitHub Secure Open Source Fund

Grant
GitHub

$10,000 per project, paid in tranches ($6,000 during the 3-week program, $2,000 at the 6-month check-in, $2,000 at the 12-month check-in), plus up to $10,000 in Azure credits

Deadline: Rolling; applications are considered for all program sessions

Funds maintainers of widely-depended-on open source projects to improve security practices, through a 3-week education program, GitHub Security Lab office hours, and direct funding tied to security milestones at 6- and 12-month check-ins. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis and considered for all program sessions.

Eligibility
Open source project maintainers (individuals or teams of up to 3) aged 18+, with an active GitHub profile, located in a GitHub Sponsors-supported region, not a current GitHub employee, with a clearly licensed project that has demonstrated community adoption and a defined governance structure.
Geography
Global (GitHub Sponsors-supported regions)
Focus area
Open source software security
Website
https://github.com/open-source/github-secure-open-source-fund

BSSw Fellowship Program

Fellowship
U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) and National Science Foundation (NSF)

Up to $25,000 per Fellow, awarded as a subcontract to the recipient's institution

Deadline: 2027 cohort applications due 2026-10-30

Provides recognition and funding to leaders and advocates of high-quality scientific software, who propose a funded activity (e.g. a workshop, tutorial, or community content) that promotes better scientific software practices.

Eligibility
Applicants at any career stage (student through senior professional) affiliated with a U.S.-based institution able to receive federal funding.
Geography
USA
Focus area
Scientific software developer productivity and sustainability
Website
https://bssw.io/pages/bssw-fellowship-program

SSI Fellowship Programme

Fellowship
Software Sustainability Institute (SSI)

£4,000 per Fellow, to spend over a 15-month period

Deadline: Applications open 10 August 2026; close 5 October 2026

Funds individuals’ plans to advance research software practices over a 15-month fellowship period (1 January 2027 – 31 March 2028), selected via a proposal and six-minute screencast presentation, with an emphasis on collaboration, communication, and practical contributions to the research software community.

Eligibility
Open to those doing work to promote good computational practices in research. Primarily for UK-based applicants or those with formal affiliation to a UK-based institution; up to three positions are available for international applicants, who must demonstrate how their plans foster international collaboration or how UK research culture benefits from their perspective/techniques.
Geography
UK (with limited international positions)
Focus area
Research software sustainability and best practices
Website
https://www.software.ac.uk/programmes/fellowship-programme/apply-fellowship-programme

CHARTED Fund 2: Community Activities

Grant
CHARTED (dRTP Skills)

£150,000 total; up to £12,500 per activity (80% of Full Economic Costs)

Deadline: Rolling deadline, 15th of alternate months; next 2026-11-15. Apply at least three months before the activity.

Supports local networks, workshops, hackathons, mentoring programmes, and training that build skills and clarify career pathways for digital Research Technical Professionals. Covers room/venue hire and catering; excludes staff time and equipment purchases.

Eligibility
UK-based researchers and dRTPs involved with research software, research data, or research computing, or freelance trainers/educators in this sector; can support non-UK collaborators visiting the UK.
Geography
UK
Focus area
Community activities for the dRTP ecosystem
Website
https://drtp-skills.ac.uk/funds-community/

CHARTED Fund 4: Professional Development

Grant
CHARTED (dRTP Skills)

£150,000 total; up to £5,000 per award

Deadline: Rolling deadline, 15th of each month

Reimbursement-based fund supporting early-career digital Research Technical Professionals to attend conferences, training courses, and certificate programmes, or undertake inter-institutional knowledge-exchange visits. Staff time is ineligible; recipients commit to sharing outcomes via blog posts or webinars.

Eligibility
Early-career professionals and those transitioning into dRTP roles, primarily UK-based; must show career benefit, plans to share outcomes locally, and have explored other funding sources first.
Geography
UK
Focus area
Professional development for dRTPs
Website
https://drtp-skills.ac.uk/funds-prof-dev/

NSF State and Regional Artificial Intelligence Infrastructure Hubs

Grant/Cooperative Agreement
National Science Foundation (NSF)

$4,000,000 – $12,000,000 per award over 5 years; ~$100,000,000 available, up to 10 awards anticipated

Deadline: Full proposal: 2026-11-04

Funds state and regional consortia (governments, institutions, industry, and philanthropy) to expand researcher access to the compute needed for AI-enabled scientific discovery. NSF supports consortium coordination, workforce development, and faculty training, while consortia arrange funding for the underlying computing resources.

Eligibility
Limited submission: one award per state or multi-state region; one proposal per institution; individuals may serve as PI, co-PI, or Senior/Key Personnel on at most one proposal per deadline. See solicitation NSF 26-513 for eligible organization types.
Geography
USA
Focus area
AI research computing infrastructure
Website
https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/us-national-science-foundation-state-regional-artificial

Training-based Workforce Development for Advanced Cyberinfrastructure (CyberTraining)

Grant
National Science Foundation (NSF)

Pilot: up to $300,000 over 2 years · Small Implementation: up to $500,000 over 4 years · Medium Implementation: up to $1,000,000 over 4 years; ~18 awards anticipated

Deadline: Full proposal: 2027-01-21 (third Thursday in January, annually thereafter)

Supports training and education that prepares the research workforce to create, use, and support advanced cyberinfrastructure — computation, large-scale data handling and analytics, networking, and security — and promotes broad adoption of CI tools and methods, including integrating computational skills into undergraduate and graduate education.

Eligibility
An individual may serve as PI, co-PI, or Senior Personnel on only one Pilot or Implementation proposal per competition. See solicitation NSF 23-520 for eligible PI types.
Geography
USA
Focus area
Research workforce training for advanced cyberinfrastructure
Website
https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/training-based-workforce-development-advanced

MIT Solve 2027 Global Challenges

Prize
MIT Solve

$10,000 grant to every selected Solver team (~$40,000 average after selection); $1M+ prize pool with individual prizes up to $150,000

Deadline: 2026-11-02 12:00 EST

MIT Solve’s open innovation challenges seek tech-based solutions from anyone, anywhere. The 2027 cycle has four challenges: Global Health, Global Climate, Global Learning, and Global Economic Prosperity. Selected Solvers receive a grant from Solve and are eligible for sponsor prizes.

Eligibility
Teams with tech-based solutions at the prototype, pilot, growth, or scale stage (concept-stage solutions are eligible only for the Indigenous Communities Fellowship); previous Solvers may apply with new solutions only. Some prizes are restricted to US-based teams.
Geography
Global
Focus area
Tech-based solutions to global challenges in health, climate, learning, and economic prosperity
Website
https://solve.mit.edu/challenges

Rapid Response Fund Vendor RFP

Contract
Open Technology Fund (OTF)

Paid vendor services; rates proposed by the vendor per the RFP

Deadline: 2026-11-23 23:59 EST (questions due 2026-10-28 23:59 EST)

OTF’s Rapid Response Fund gives recipients either direct financial support or technical services from trusted vendors. This RFP is looking for vendors who can provide emergency digital security support in selected service categories, helping independent media, civil society, and at-risk individuals or organizations hit by state-sponsored digital attacks stay safe, get back online, prevent future attacks, and respond to sudden censorship events.

Eligibility
Service providers, organizations, and individuals, mostly digital security practitioners, with technical, thematic, or regional expertise in responding to digital emergencies. Submissions go to rapidresponse@opentech.fund and must follow the RFP’s “Proposal Submission” guidelines.
Geography
Global
Focus area
Emergency digital security support for independent media and civil society facing state-sponsored digital attacks or censorship
Website
https://www.opentech.fund/news/open-technology-fund-requests-proposals-for-the-rapid-response-fund/
